**method** · FLIPR 2026 · explicit

Step 2: FLIPR estimates the national environment as a weighted average of the generic ballot and a 'bottom-up' estimate implied by polls of individual Senate and House races relative to each race's partisan lean. ### 2026-08-11 · [How FLIPR forecasts the midterm elections](https://www.natesilver.net/p/flipr-midterms-model-methodology)
**model change** · FLIPR 2026 · explicit

New in 2026, the bottom-up national estimate gives more weight to states and districts that resemble most others nationally, so a poll of a Pennsylvania swing district counts more than one in Hawaii or rural Alabama. It also corrects for selection bias in which races get polled.

*Why:* Competitive-looking races in lopsided seats get polled more, which biases naive estimates.

> a poll of a swing district in Pennsylvania will have more impact on the calculation than a race in Hawaii or rural Alabama.
