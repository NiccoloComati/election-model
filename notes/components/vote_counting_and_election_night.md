**assumption** · FiveThirtyEight 2020 presidential · explicit

The model forecasts the vote as cast, assuming reasonable efforts to let eligible citizens vote and count legal ballots and that electors go to each state's popular-vote winner; it does not model extraconstitutional interference. Its probabilities can still serve as a benchmark for suspicious claims, such as a candidate claiming a state where it gave them 0.4 percent.

### 2024-11-26 · [SBSQ #15: Democrats have a "fool me twice" problem](https://www.natesilver.net/p/sbsq-15-democrats-have-a-fool-me)
**limitation or failure mode** · 2024 election night model · explicit

The 2024 Silver Bulletin election night model was turned off after a few hours on Nov. 5, 2024. Silver says it needed another full day or two of testing for bugs and for whether its results passed the smell test under realistic scenarios.

*Numbers:* additional testing time needed = a full day or two days (testing the election night model before Nov. 5, 2024)

*Why:* On election night, when something breaks there isn't enough time to diagnose and fix it.

> We probably needed to budget another full day or two of testing for the election night model, both to look for bugs and to see if it was producing results that passed the smell test
