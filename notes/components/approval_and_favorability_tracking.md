### 2024-10-22 · [Likability isn't enough](https://www.natesilver.net/p/likability-isnt-enough)
**interpretation guidance** · general principle · explicit

Silver treats a candidate's advantage in favorability ratings as, at most, a minor factor that might suggest room for late growth among undecided voters. He does not expect a gap between favorability and head-to-head polls to close. The 2016 precedent (Clinton led more in favorability than in head-to-head polls and still lost) argues against using the favorability gap as a predictor.

*Why:* In 2016 Clinton had a similar favorability-vs-head-to-head gap and lost. Favorability measures likability, while votes depend on perceived policy and who 'fights for you'.

> I don't particularly think there's any reason to expect that gap to close.

### 2025-03-22 · [How low is Trump's popularity floor?](https://www.natesilver.net/p/how-low-is-trumps-popularity-floor)
**empirical regularity** · general principle · explicit

A new president's disapproval rating typically rises early in the term. Part of the reason is that voters who were initially undecided, perhaps out of hopefulness, start telling pollsters they disapprove. Approval may stay relatively flat while disapproval rises. This happened for all recent presidents, including Trump in 2025.

*Why:* Undecided respondents give a new president the benefit of the doubt, then break toward disapproval.

> some voters who were initially on the fence and say they were undecided, perhaps out of a sense of hopefulness for a new president, instead begin to tell pollsters they disapprove

**method** · Silver Bulletin historical approval database · explicit

Silver summarizes a presidential term's approval as follows:
- The average approval uses the final tracker value at the end of each day of the term.
- The range is the 95th percentile range of those daily numbers, used to define the 'floor' and 'ceiling'. This throws out extremely short-lived fluctuations.

*Numbers:* percentile range = 95 percent (range of daily approval values used to define floor/ceiling)

*Why:* Excludes extremely short-lived fluctuations.

### 2026-11-04 · [How popular is Donald Trump?](https://www.natesilver.net/p/trump-approval-ratings-nate-silver-bulletin)
**method** · Silver Bulletin approval average 2026 · explicit

For the presidential approval average, when a pollster releases multiple versions, Silver Bulletin prefers the all-adult version, then registered voters, then likely voters, because all Americans have a say in how popular the president is. This is the opposite of the generic ballot preference.

*Why:* Approval measures the whole public, not just voters.

> If a pollster releases multiple versions of the same survey, we use the all-adult version of the poll before the registered voter version.
