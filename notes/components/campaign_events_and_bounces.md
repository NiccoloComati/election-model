*Why:* Event bounces often fade.

### 2026-10-15 · [Welcome to midterm silly season 🤡](https://www.natesilver.net/p/welcome-to-midterm-silly-season)
**empirical regularity** · historical · explicit

Most elections, and nearly all midterms, lack late focal-point events. Exceptions with dynamic finishes were 2008 (financial crisis, debates) and 2016 (the Comey letter on Oct. 28, a canonical October Surprise). In 2024 the drama was concentrated in a 10-week summer window.

*Why:* Midterms are hundreds of separate elections with no single focal point.

> Since they consist of what are technically hundreds of separate elections across the country, it’s hard to have focal point moments like you can get in a presidential year.
