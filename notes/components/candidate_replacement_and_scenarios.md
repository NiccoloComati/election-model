### 2023-09-18 · [Is a Trump-Biden rematch inevitable?](https://www.natesilver.net/p/is-a-trump-biden-rematch-inevitable)
**method** · Silver judgmental estimate 2023 · explicit

Silver estimates nomination probabilities by summing the probabilities of distinct failure scenarios. For Trump: about 10% for losing in 'typical' fashion (for example, caught from behind in Iowa), 5% for a health issue, and 10% for legal jeopardy forcing withdrawal or a voter turn. That totals roughly 25%, so Trump has about a 75% nomination chance.

*Numbers:* typical primary loss = 10 percent (Trump 2024); health issue = 5 percent (Trump 2024); legal jeopardy = 10 percent (Trump 2024); Trump nomination probability = 75 percent (Silver estimate, Sept 2023)

*Why:* Decomposing risk into scenarios makes market prices easier to judge.

> That gets me to about a 75 percent chance of his being nominated again.

### 2023-09-18 · [Is a Trump-Biden rematch inevitable?](https://www.natesilver.net/p/is-a-trump-biden-rematch-inevitable)
**method** · Silver judgmental estimate 2023 · explicit

For Biden, Silver decomposes non-nomination risk as follows: roughly 10% for death or a significant medical event forcing withdrawal, a smaller chance of voluntary withdrawal (LBJ-style), and an even smaller chance that a new entrant defeats him. *Numbers:* medical event forcing withdrawal = 10 percent (Biden 2024); non-Biden nominee = 15 or 20 percent (Silver estimate); Biden nomination probability = 80 to 85 percent (Silver estimate, Sept 2023)

> That’s how I get to a 15 or 20 percent chance of someone other than Biden being the nominee.

### 2023-09-18 · [Is a Trump-Biden rematch inevitable?](https://www.natesilver.net/p/is-a-trump-biden-rematch-inevitable)
**method** · general principle · explicit

For candidate health risk, Silver starts from an actuarial base rate: about 5% one-year mortality for an average 80-year-old non-Hispanic white man. He adjusts it down for Biden's better-than-average health and medical care, and shortens the horizon because the convention is 11 months away. He then adds non-fatal disabling events such as strokes, arriving at about 10% combined.

*Numbers:* one-year mortality base rate = about 5 percent (average non-Hispanic white man aged 80); time to convention = 11 months (Democratic convention); death or disabling medical event = 10 percent (Biden)

*Why:* Non-fatal events like strokes would also likely end a campaign.

> The chance of an average non-Hispanic white man of Biden’s age (80) dying in the next year are about 5 percent.

### 2024-07-05 · [The broken leg problem](https://www.natesilver.net/p/the-broken-leg-problem)
**empirical regularity** · 2024 presidential · explicit

Polling often changes drastically once a candidate goes from hypothetical to actual (a point credited to Nate Cohn). Hypothetical matchup polls are therefore a poor basis for forecasting a replacement candidate.

*Why:* There is no relevant empirical data for an unprecedented replacement scenario.

> the polling often changes drastically once a candidate goes from a hypothetical candidate to an actual one.
